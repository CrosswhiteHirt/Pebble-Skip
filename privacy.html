Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Short version: Pebble Skip is an offline game. It does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or track personal data. Game progress and preferences are stored only on your device.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Jiale Game handles information in the Pebble Skip mobile application and on its related informational website. It applies to all users of Pebble Skip.

2. Data We Collect

Pebble Skip does not collect personal data or device data. The app does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, identifiers, diagnostics, usage analytics, advertising data, payment information, or gameplay data from your device.

3. Local Game Data

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Best distance, score, skip count, and Perfect combo.
  • Level unlocks, completion results, and stars.
  • Music, sound, and haptic feedback preferences.

This information is stored using Apple platform storage and is not transmitted to Jiale Game or any third party.

4. Tracking, Advertising, and Analytics

Pebble Skip does not use advertising networks, analytics services, tracking technologies, cookies, cross-app tracking, or third-party login services. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission because it does not track users.

5. Device Permissions

The app does not request access to location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, Bluetooth, health data, or other protected personal information. Optional haptic feedback uses the device vibration system without collecting data.

6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Jiale Game does not sell, rent, disclose, or share user data because the app does not collect it. The app does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. If this changes, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed version is released.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until the app data is removed. You can delete all locally stored Pebble Skip data by deleting the app from your device. Because Jiale Game does not receive or store this information, there is no server-side account or data record to retrieve or delete.

8. Consent and Privacy Choices

Pebble Skip does not ask for consent to collect personal data because no personal data is collected. Music, sound, and haptic feedback can be changed at any time in the in-game Settings screen. You may stop all local processing by deleting the app.

9. Children's Privacy

Pebble Skip does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The game contains no account creation, communication feature, advertising, behavioral tracking, or user-generated content.

10. Security

The app minimizes privacy risk by operating offline and retaining game progress only on the device. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, but Pebble Skip does not maintain a remote database of user information.

11. Website Information

The Pebble Skip informational pages do not intentionally set cookies or use analytics. A hosting provider may process routine server logs, such as IP address and request time, under its own terms and privacy policy. Jiale Game does not use those logs to profile Pebble Skip players.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. The revised policy will show a new effective date. Material changes to data practices will also be reflected in the app and App Store privacy disclosures before they take effect.
